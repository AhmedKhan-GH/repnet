  \section{Motivation, Background, and Context}
  Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disorder in pregnant women that accounts for 2-8\% of pregnancy complications, over 50,000 maternal deaths, and over 500,000 fetal deaths yearly worldwide \cite{nbk570611}.

  \section{Problem Definition}

 Early diagnosis is essential but solely relying on the onset of symptoms that warrant testing and treatment is insufficient. Analysis of readily available data such as ECG waveforms demands expert man-hours rationed by warrant so as to not waste resources on false positives.

  \section{Outline of Your Solution}

 The goal of this project is to develop a machine learning model that can predict the risk of preeclampsia from raw ECG waveforms to warrant testing and treatment for before onset of symptoms, and to create a system to augment clinical workflows in the identification of indicative waveform features. Furthermore, our goal is to analyze the model's findings of which ECG leads are relevant through dimensionality reduction, and perform model compression that enables effective deployment in edge devices for the given privacy protections and compute constraints of medical practice.
