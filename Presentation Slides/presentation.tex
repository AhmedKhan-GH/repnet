      \begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
        \item \textbf{Baseline Wander Removal:} 4th-order Butterworth high-pass at 0.5~Hz
        \item \textbf{Powerline Interference:} 60~Hz IIR notch filter ($Q=30$)
        \item \textbf{Z-Score Normalization:} Per-lead $\frac{x - \mu_\ell}{\sigma_\ell + \epsilon}$
      \end{enumerate}
    \end{column}

    \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
      {\small\Raleway\bfseries\color{aggieblue} Training Configuration}\\[0.3em]
      \usebeamerfont{footline}\color{coolgray}
      \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
        \item \textbf{AdamW} + cosine annealing (lr $= 1.2\!\times\!10^{-3}$)
        \item Dropout 0.15, weight decay $5\!\times\!10^{-3}$
        \item Focal loss ($\gamma = 1.0$) + label smoothing 0.05
        \item Early stop on val AUROC, patience 20, 80 epochs
        \item \textbf{30-split} patient-grouped validation (80/20)
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}

  \end{columns}

  \vspace{0.3em}
  {\small\Raleway\bfseries\color{aggieblue}\centering 7-Transform On-the-Fly Augmentation\par}\vspace{0.2em}
  \resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
    \begin{tabular}{lllp{8cm}}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Transform} & \textbf{Prob.} & \textbf{Range} & \textbf{Description} \\
      \midrule
      Gaussian Noise & 50\% & $\sigma \in [0.01, 0.05]$ & Simulates sensor/environmental noise \\
      Amplitude Scale & 50\% & $\pm 15\%$ & Models gain variation across devices \\
      Time Shift & 30\% & $\pm 150$ samples & Circular shift ($\pm$0.6\,s at 250\,Hz) \\
      Lead Dropout & 10\% & 12\% per lead & Zeroes individual leads; forces cross-lead redundancy \\
      Cutout & 25\% & 50--200 samples & Zeros a contiguous random segment \\
      Baseline Wander & 20\% & 0.05--0.5~Hz & Injects low-frequency sinusoid \\
      Speed Perturb & 10\% & $\pm 5\%$ & Resample to stretch/compress; models HR variability \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}

\end{frame}

      {\small\Raleway\bfseries\color{aggieblue} LLM-Driven Experiment Loop}\\[0.3em]
      An autonomous AI agent (Karpathy's \textit{autoresearch} framework) iteratively edited the model code, trained, and evaluated --- keeping improvements and discarding regressions:
      \begin{enumerate}
        \item Modify \texttt{train.py} with a new architectural idea
        \item Train for up to 80 epochs (10-min wall-clock budget)
        \item Evaluate on held-out validation split
        \item If AUROC improves $\to$ keep; otherwise $\to$ discard
        \item Repeat (250 experiments total)
      \end{enumerate}

        \item Inception, SincNet, CWT dual-path
